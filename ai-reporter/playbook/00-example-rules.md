# Example Trading Rules (Delete or Modify as Needed)

## Risk Sentiment Rules

When EEM (Emerging Markets ETF) is bearish, reduce confidence in all commodity currencies (AUD, NZD, CAD) by 1 full grade. EM capital flight precedes EM currency weakness by 24-48 hours.

When VIX > 25 and VVIX > 110, immediately shift from NEUTRAL to RISK-OFF bias. Pre-panic conditions override normal macro analysis. Reduce size on ALL risk-on trades.

## USD Direction Rules

When Fed is hawkish AND real yields are rising, USD bulls get +1 confidence adjustment. This is the most powerful structural USD signal in modern forex. Pairs are 2x more likely to move in the direction of USD bias.

When Fed is dovish but liquidity is contracting (QT regime), don't trust the dovish bias. QT structural headwind dominates. Mark USD pairs as WATCH, not clear directional bets.

## Intermarket Divergence Rules

SPX up but HYG (high-yield bonds) down = fake rally. Smart money is exiting. Reduce RISK-ON bias immediately. AUD/USD and NZD/USD BUY signals become WATCH or REJECTED.

NDX (Nasdaq) up but SMH (Semis) down = hollow tech rally. This divergence leads AUD/NZD reversals by 1-2 weeks. If we see this, downgrade AUD/NZD grades by 1 notch.

## Grade Rules

Only trade A or B grade signals with full conviction. C grades should be WATCH or SKIP, never BUY/SELL. When kill switches are borderline (killer yield alignment issues), skip the trade entirely.

## Timing Rules

Don't trade any pair if danger zone is active. Calendars trump everything. The cost of being right is not worth being caught in a high-impact announcement.

If a pair has r2InversionNote (R2 Lie Detector warning), treat it as maximum +1 confidence. R2 warnings are early-stage signals, not confirmations.
